# Checkout UI and UX

PowerPay Beta 1 now includes three merchant payment experiences:

## Merchant checkout

- Client selection and search
- Asset selection
- Payment amount, memo, and recipient review
- Merchant verification badges
- Fee and settlement summary
- Light and dark themes using forest green accents

## Client QR payments

- Client-specific QR payment request
- Editable amount, asset, and memo
- Copy-link and QR-download actions
- Merchant-branded scan experience

## PowerPOS

- In-person amount keypad
- Customer context
- QR, NFC, and payment-link methods
- Automatic receipt status
- Instant merchant settlement messaging

The design avoids blue accents and uses forest green throughout both themes.
